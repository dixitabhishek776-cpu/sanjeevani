# Clinical Review Packet

This packet is for independent professional review; it is not a claim of clinical validation.

Review areas:
- crisis tripwires and classifier policy
- response language
- escalation thresholds
- intervention catalog
- cultural/language coverage
- adolescent/minor handling
- false-positive burden
- false-negative risk
- human reviewer SOP
- resource accuracy and freshness

Reviewers should sign off on versioned artifacts and record open risks.
