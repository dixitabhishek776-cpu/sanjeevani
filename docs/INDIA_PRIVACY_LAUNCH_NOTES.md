# India Privacy Launch Notes

For an India-first launch, counsel should map Sanjeevani against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

Engineering controls already represented include:
- explicit opt-in preferences;
- versioned consent records;
- user data export;
- account deletion/deactivation;
- encryption and access control;
- audit logging;
- incident response.

The final privacy notice must clearly identify data categories, purposes, retention and user controls. This document is not legal advice.
