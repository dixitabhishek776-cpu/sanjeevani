# Sanjeevani Incident Response

## Severity
- **SEV-0:** active safety failure, data exposure, or unauthorized access.
- **SEV-1:** material security defect, prolonged outage, or broken crisis routing.
- **SEV-2:** degraded non-safety functionality.
- **SEV-3:** minor defect.

## Response
1. Detect and open an incident record.
2. Assign an incident commander.
3. Contain the affected feature/service.
4. Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
5. Assess user, safety and data impact.
6. Notify appropriate parties under applicable law/contracts.
7. Restore safe operation.
8. Conduct a blameless post-incident review.
9. Add regression tests and corrective actions.

For India, review breach response against the DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025, including their phased commencement dates.
