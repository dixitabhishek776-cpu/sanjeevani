# Clinical & Safety Release Gate

Sanjeevani must not be publicly launched as a mental-health support product until accountable humans sign off on:

- Clinical review of intervention content and crisis flows.
- Lived-experience review, including youth considerations if minors are in scope.
- Independent evaluation of false negatives and false positives.
- English, Hindi and Hinglish evaluation.
- Adversarial and prompt-injection evaluation.
- A staffed human-escalation SOP.
- Emergency-resource verification for every launch country.
- Clear disclosure that AI is not a replacement for emergency/professional care.
- Safety-incident and model-drift monitoring.
- Rollback capability for model/prompt/content changes.

WHO's March 2026 responsible-AI discussion emphasizes multidisciplinary co-design, evidence grounding, cultural/linguistic tailoring, independent evaluation and accountability for AI used around mental-health vulnerability.
